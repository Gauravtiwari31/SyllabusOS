The problem

Students aren't short of content. They're short of decisions.

No prioritisation30+ topics, no order, no weightage, no link to the exam date.
No self-knowledge"I read it" isn't "I know it". The gap shows up in the exam hall.
Plans break on day twoMiss one session and a fixed timetable becomes a backlog.
Chatbots make it worsePaste the question, copy the answer, feel ready, fail the offline exam.
